Help Topics

Quick answers to common questions about the app.

How do I get an account?

Accounts are created by the mosque office, not by self sign-up. If you do not have one yet, contact the office and they will register you.

I forgot my password

On the login screen tap Forgot Password?, enter your account email and tap Send Reset Link.

The email can land in spam. If the link will not open, mark the message as “Not spam” first, or copy the link into your browser. Each link works once.

How do I change my password?

Open Profile → Change Password. Enter your current password, then the new one twice.

How do I change my sign-in email?

Open Profile → Edit Profile → Change Email. Your sign-in email changes as soon as you save; use the new address the next time you sign in.

Can I change the language or text size?

Yes. In Profile, the Accessibility section lets you choose the language (English or Urdu), the font size and high contrast. Dark Mode is there too.

A detail in my record is wrong

You can update your name and contact details in Profile → Edit Profile. For anything else, write to the office and ask for it to be corrected.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Delete Account → Request Account Deletion, or email aishamasjid.ot@gmail.com from the address on your account. See what is removed and what is kept.
