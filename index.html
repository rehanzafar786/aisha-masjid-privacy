01

Introduction

Aisha Masjid uses this app to record the donations entrusted to the mosque and to show donors how those funds are used.

This policy explains what personal information the app holds, why it is held, and the choices available to you. It applies to everyone who signs in to the Aisha Masjid app, whether as a donor, an accountant or an administrator.

02

Information We Collect

Accounts are created by the mosque office, not by self sign-up. The app holds the following:

Account and personal information

  • Name, email address and phone number
  • CNIC, gender and postal address
  • Monthly commitment and the accountant assigned to you

You sign in with your email address and a password that you alone know.

Donation records

The donations recorded against your name: the amount, date, method, receipt number and the project a contribution was tagged to.

Photos you choose to attach

Any profile photo or receipt image you choose to attach. Photos are only taken from your device when you choose to attach one.

Device and technical information

When you sign in, a notification token for your device so announcements and receipts can reach you, plus basic technical details such as the app version and your chosen language and accessibility settings.

Location information Not collected

The app does not track your location. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.

03

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to run the mosque's donation records:

  • To identify you when you sign in
  • To record and acknowledge your contributions
  • To issue receipts
  • To let your assigned accountant collect your monthly commitment
  • To send announcements and reminders about mosque events and projects
  • To prepare the accounts the mosque is obliged to keep
04

Information Sharing

Your personal details are visible only to:

Youyour own record
Your accountantthe one assigned to you
Administratorof the mosque
Other donorsnever see your details

Published fund-utilisation figures are aggregated totals and never name an individual donor.

Your information is never sold, rented or shared for marketing.

05

Third-Party Services

The app stores data with these service providers:

G

Google Firebase

Authentication, database and notifications · Privacy information

C

Cloudflare

File and image storage · Privacy policy

These providers process the data on the mosque's behalf under their own security obligations and may store it on servers outside your country. No other third party receives your data, except where the mosque is required to disclose it by law.

06

Data Storage and Security

Access is protected by an email and password you alone know.

Data is encrypted in transit.

Access rules limit each account to the records its role is permitted to see.

Please keep your password private and tell the mosque office at once if you think someone else has used your account.

07

Data Retention

  1. While your account is open

    Profile details are kept for as long as your account is open.

  2. At least seven years

    Donation and receipt records are kept for at least seven years after they are recorded, because the mosque must be able to account for every amount it has received. These records are retained in an anonymised form even after an account is closed.

08

Account and Data Deletion

You can ask for your account and personal data to be deleted in either of two ways:

  1. In the app: open Profile → Delete Account → Request Account Deletion. You are asked to type the email address you are signed in with to confirm, and the request is sent to the mosque office.
  2. By email: write to aishamasjid.ot@gmail.com from the email address on your account.

What will be removed

  • Your sign-in and password
  • Your name, email, phone, CNIC and address
  • Your profile photo and notification settings

What will be kept

Receipts and accounting entries for donations already recorded are kept, without your personal details attached, for at least seven years from the date each amount was recorded. These anonymised records cannot be traced back to you.

09

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for adults who contribute to the mosque. Accounts are not knowingly created for children under 13, and any such account found will be removed.

10

Your Rights

You may ask:

See

To see the information held about you.

Correct

To have an inaccurate detail corrected.

Delete

To have your account closed and your personal data deleted.

The Delete Account screen in the app starts a deletion request (see Account and Data Deletion), or you may contact the mosque office directly.

11

Policy Updates

If this policy changes, the revised version will appear on this page and on the Privacy Policy screen in the app, with a new “Last updated” date. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.

12

Contact Information
